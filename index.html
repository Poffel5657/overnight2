        How to Play

        CLICK: walk, talk, attack. RIGHT-CLICK / HOLD: all actions.

        WASD: walk (relative to the camera). TAB: next target. 1-9: abilities (change keys under CHAR > KEYS). ARROWS / Q E / MIDDLE-DRAG: turn camera. WHEEL: zoom.

        C: character. B: bag. J: quests. ENTER: say something (a bubble over your head, gone after 5 seconds). Talk to Warden Ama by the Beacon to begin.

        Your lantern burns oil out in the Grey at night. Fill it at the Beacon.

        Day and night follow the real clock in Kindle (Berlin time). At midnight the world grows.

        Greylings are bold in the dark. Light makes them shy.

        PROTOTYPE. Nothing is saved yet.

        UI size, sound and CRT: Settings (gear button or O). Drag windows by their title to move them.
